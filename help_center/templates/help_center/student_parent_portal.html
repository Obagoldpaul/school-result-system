Student & Parent Portal

Learn how to access student information, view academic records, check attendance, review school fees and access payment information.

1. Accessing the Student Portal

The Student Portal provides students with access to their personal, academic, attendance and financial information.

  1. Open your school's Paul SchoolHub login page.
  2. Enter your assigned username and password.
  3. Select the login option to access your dashboard.
  4. After logging in, use the Student Portal navigation menu to access the available sections.
Important: The current portal uses student accounts. It does not provide a separate parent login or independent parent dashboard. Parents and guardians can review the available information with the student using the student's account.

2. Viewing My Profile

The My Profile section displays the personal information associated with the student's account.

The information may include:

  • Passport photograph.
  • Full name.
  • Admission number.
  • Class and department.
  • Date of birth and gender.
  • Nationality.
  • Guardian's name and phone number.

If any information is missing or incorrect, contact the school administration for assistance.

3. Viewing Academic Information and Subjects

The Academic Summary section provides an overview of the student's academic records.

It displays:

  • The number of registered subjects.
  • The publication status of the latest results.
  • A link to view registered subjects.
  • A link to view report cards.
Viewing My Subjects
  1. Select My Subjects from the academic section of the dashboard.
  2. Review the total number of registered subjects.
  3. Check the list of compulsory subjects.
  4. Check the list of selected elective subjects.

The page also displays the student's name, admission number, class and department.

If an expected subject is missing, contact the school administration for clarification.

4. Viewing My Report Card

Students can use the report card section to view their academic performance for available academic terms.

  1. Select My Report Card from the Academics menu or Academic Summary section.
  2. Choose the academic term you want to view.
  3. Select View Report Card.
  4. Review the displayed academic results and performance information.

A report card may contain:

  • Student and school information.
  • Subject scores, grades and remarks.
  • Continuous Assessment and examination scores.
  • Overall and cumulative performance.
  • Class position, where enabled by the school.
  • Social habits and behavioural ratings.
  • Attendance summary.
  • Class teacher's comment and principal's remark.
Note: Students can only view results after the school's results for the relevant class and term have been fully published.

5. Viewing My Timetable

The My Timetable section displays the published timetable for the student's current class.

  1. Select My Timetable from the Student Portal navigation menu.
  2. Check the academic session and term displayed.
  3. Review the timetable by day and period.

The timetable may show:

  • Days of the school week.
  • Period names and times.
  • Subjects scheduled for each period.
  • Assigned teachers.
  • Break periods.
The timetable must be published by the school before it becomes available to students.

6. Checking My Attendance

The My Attendance section provides an overview of the student's attendance for the current term.

The dashboard displays:

  • Days Present.
  • Days Marked.
  • Attendance Rate.
  • Current Term.
Viewing Attendance History
  1. Open the My Attendance section.
  2. Select View Attendance History.
  3. Review the attendance summary.
  4. Check individual attendance records by date, day and status.

Attendance records show whether the student was marked Present or Absent.

When the current term has configured start and end dates, the history page displays records within that term. Otherwise, it displays the available attendance history.

7. Checking My School Fee Account

The My School Fee Account section provides an overview of the student's school fee obligations and payment position.

Current Term Summary

The dashboard displays:

  • Current Term Charges: Total fees assigned to the student for the current term.
  • Current Term Paid: Payments allocated towards the student's current term charges.
  • Current Term Balance: The remaining balance for the current term.
Total Account Arrears

The account position section displays the student's total outstanding arrears, including current and previous outstanding balances.

Viewing the Full Account Statement
  1. Select View Full Account Statement.
  2. Review the student's financial summary.
  3. Check charges, allocated payments and outstanding balances across academic sessions.
  4. Review any opening balance information where applicable.
Downloading the School Bill

Select Download School Bill to download the student's fee bill for the displayed term.

The school fee account reflects fee assignments and payments recorded by the school.

8. Viewing Payment History and Receipts

The Payment History section provides access to payment records associated with the student's school fee account.

  1. Open the Payment History section on the dashboard.
  2. Select View My Payment History.
  3. Review the available payment records.

Payment records may display:

  • Payment date.
  • Amount paid.
  • Academic term.
  • Payment method.
  • Payment reference.
  • Receipt action.
Printing a Payment Receipt

Select Print Receipt beside a payment record to download its PDF receipt.

Payment records and receipts accessed through the student portal are restricted to the student's own account.

9. Viewing School Payment Information

When the school has configured its payment details, the dashboard displays a School Payment Account section.

The information may include:

  • Bank name.
  • Account name.
  • Account number.
Important: Always confirm the displayed payment details before making a bank transfer. Contact the school if you are unsure about any payment instruction.

10. Information for Parents and Guardians

The student's profile includes parent or guardian information, such as the guardian's name and phone number, where these details have been provided to the school.

Parents and guardians can review the student's available academic, attendance and fee information together with the student through the student's portal account.

The current system provides a student-account-based portal. For account access, corrections to student information or questions about school records, contact the school administration.
Access and availability: The availability of portal pages depends on the school's enabled features and the student's account. If a page is unavailable or information appears incorrect, contact the school administration for assistance.
