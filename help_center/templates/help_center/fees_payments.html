Fees & Payments

Learn how to configure school fees, assign charges to students, record payments, manage outstanding balances and review financial reports.

1. Managing Fee Categories

Fee categories help your school organise the different charges students are expected to pay. Examples may include tuition, examination fees, development levies and other school charges.

Creating a Fee Category
  1. Open Fee Categories from the Billing section.
  2. Select Add Fee Category.
  3. Enter the fee category name.
  4. Select whether the category is Compulsory or Optional.
  5. Set its active status as appropriate.
  6. Save the category.
Understanding Fee Types
  • Compulsory fees: These are automatically included when calculating applicable student charges.
  • Optional fees: These are included for students who have opted in. Their enrolment can be managed individually.

The category list displays each category's name, type, number of assignments and active status. Authorised users can edit categories or activate and deactivate them.

Note: Fee categories must be created before they can be selected when creating fee assignments.

2. Creating and Managing Fee Assignments

A fee assignment specifies how much a student or group of students is expected to pay for a particular fee category during an academic term.

Creating a Fee Assignment
  1. Open Fee Assignments.
  2. Select Add Fee Assignment.
  3. Choose the fee category.
  4. Select the academic session.
  5. Select the term.
  6. Choose the assignment target:
    • An individual student; or
    • A class, optionally restricted to a department.
  7. Enter the fee amount.
  8. Set the assignment's active status.
  9. Save the assignment.
Important: An assignment can target an individual student or a class. You cannot select an individual student together with a class or department. A department must be selected together with a class.
Viewing and Filtering Assignments

The Fee Assignments page allows you to filter records by academic session, term, fee category, fee type and active status. You can also search using student names, admission numbers, classes or fee categories.

Depending on the assignment, you can edit its details or manage the students enrolled in an optional fee.

Managing Optional Fee Enrolment
  1. Locate the optional fee assignment.
  2. Select Manage Students.
  3. Review the students covered by the assignment.
  4. Use Remove to opt a student out.
  5. Use Opt In to include a student again.
Default behaviour: Students are treated as opted in when no enrolment record exists for an optional fee. You can change this using the Manage Students page.

3. Recording Student Payments

Authorised billing users can record payments made by students or their parents and allocate the money to outstanding fee categories.

Recording a Payment
  1. Open Students Owing or the relevant student fee payment page.
  2. Locate the student and select the payment action.
  3. Review the student's fee breakdown.
  4. Enter the amount received.
  5. Select the payment method.
  6. Enter the transaction reference, where applicable.
  7. Add a note if necessary.
  8. Allocate the payment to the relevant fee categories.
  9. Select Distribute Automatically if you want the system to distribute the amount across the displayed outstanding categories.
  10. Review the allocation summary and select Record Payment.
Understanding Payment Allocation

Payments must be fully allocated before they can be recorded. The total allocated amount must equal the amount received, and an allocation cannot exceed the outstanding balance of its fee category.

Important: Always verify the amount received and its allocation before saving. The system validates the allocation to prevent overpayment of individual fee categories.

After a successful payment, the system displays the payment details and receipt number. You can proceed to print the receipt or return to Students Owing.

4. Viewing Payment History and Receipts

The Payment History page provides a record of payments recorded by the school.

Each record displays the payment date, student name, admission number, term, amount, payment method, receipt number and staff member who recorded it.

Available Payment Actions
  • View: Opens the payment receipt.
  • PDF: Downloads the payment receipt as a PDF.
  • Manage Allocation: Allows authorised users to adjust how the payment is distributed across fee categories.
Note: Managing an allocation changes how an existing payment is distributed. It does not change the original payment amount.

5. Managing Opening Balances

Opening balances are used to record outstanding arrears that existed before a student began using the school's billing system.

Adding an Opening Balance
  1. Open Add Opening Balance.
  2. Search for the student using their name, admission number or class.
  3. Select the correct student.
  4. Enter the outstanding amount.
  5. Add a note explaining the balance, if necessary.
  6. Save the record.
Viewing and Managing Opening Balances

The Opening Balances page displays the student, original balance, amount paid and remaining balance. You can search for students and use the available actions to pay, view payment history, edit or delete an opening balance.

Recording an Opening Balance Payment
  1. Locate the student on the Opening Balances page.
  2. Select Pay.
  3. Review the original balance, amount already paid and remaining balance.
  4. Enter the amount received.
  5. Select the payment method.
  6. Enter the transaction reference and any relevant note.
  7. Select Record Payment.

You can view the payment history and open or download individual opening balance payment receipts.

Important: Opening balances should only be used for arrears that existed before the student began using the billing system. Do not use them as a substitute for normal term fee assignments.

6. Viewing Students Owing

The Students Owing page helps school management and authorised billing staff identify students with outstanding school fee balances.

Filtering the Owing List
  1. Open Students Owing.
  2. Select an academic session.
  3. Select the relevant term.
  4. Optionally select a class.
  5. Apply the filters to view the relevant records.

The page displays student details, term fees, payments, term balance, outstanding terms and total account arrears.

Available Actions
  • Pay: Record a payment for the student.
  • Bill PDF: Download the student's fee bill.
  • History: View the student's payment history.
  • Statement: View the student's account statement.
Printing the Students Owing Report

Use Print Students Owing to generate a printable report for the selected session and term. You can print the whole school's list or restrict the report to a selected class.

The printed report includes students with outstanding account arrears, their term balances, outstanding terms and opening arrears.

Printing Class Bills

When a class is selected, the available Print Class Bills action generates a consolidated PDF showing the active students in that class, their total fees, payments and balances for the selected term.

7. Viewing and Downloading Student Fee Bills

A student fee bill shows the charges assigned to a student for a selected academic term.

Selecting a Student Fee Bill
  1. Open Student Fee Bill.
  2. Select the academic session.
  3. Select the term.
  4. Select the student's class.
  5. Choose the student from the available list.
  6. Select View Fee Bill.

The bill displays the school's details, student information, academic term and a breakdown of fee categories.

Each fee category shows the assigned amount, amount paid, outstanding balance and payment status. The bill also displays the total fees, total paid and total outstanding balance.

Use the PDF option to download the student's fee bill.

8. Viewing Student Account Statements

A student account statement provides a consolidated financial history across academic sessions.

You can open a student's statement from the Students Owing page or use the Account Statements option on the Billing Dashboard to select a student.

Understanding the Statement

The statement contains three main summary figures:

  • Total Charges: Fees charged to the student.
  • Total Payments Allocated: Payments distributed to those charges.
  • Total Account Arrears: The outstanding account balance.

Below the summary, charges, allocated payments and arrears are grouped by academic session and term. Where an opening balance exists, the statement also displays the original amount, payments made and remaining opening arrears.

Important accounting information: Account statements calculate outstanding fees using allocated payments. A payment that has not been allocated to fee categories does not automatically reduce the displayed fee arrears.

The statement also provides a link to the student's payment history.

9. Viewing Financial Reports

Financial reports help school management review collections, outstanding fees and student account balances.

Term Financial Report
  1. Open Term Financial Report from the Billing Dashboard.
  2. Select the academic session.
  3. Select the term.
  4. Select View Report.

The report includes:

  • Expected revenue, amount collected and outstanding balance.
  • Collection rate and number of active students.
  • Number of students owing.
  • Fee category breakdown.
  • Payment count and payment-method summary.
  • Student financial summaries grouped by class.
  • Unallocated payment amounts.
Session Financial Report
  1. Open Session Financial Report.
  2. Select the academic session.
  3. Select View Report.

This report provides a financial overview of the entire academic session, including a term-by-term breakdown, fee category totals, payment summaries, student financial summaries and overall totals.

Printing Financial Reports

Both financial reports include a Print Report button for printing the displayed report.

Understanding unallocated payments: Financial reports show collected payments separately from amounts allocated to student fee categories. This helps identify money received that has not yet been distributed against specific fee charges.
Access and permissions: Billing pages are available according to your school's subscription features and your assigned account permissions. Some actions, such as managing fee assignments, recording payments and viewing financial reports, require different permissions. Students can access their own available payment information through the student portal where the relevant portal feature is enabled.
