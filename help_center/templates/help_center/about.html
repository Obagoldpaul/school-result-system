About Paul SchoolHub

A smarter way to organise and manage school operations.

What is Paul SchoolHub?

Paul SchoolHub is a web-based School Management System developed to help schools manage their academic and administrative activities from one central platform.

It provides digital tools for managing students, teachers, classes, subjects, attendance, academic results, school fees, announcements, timetables and other essential school operations.

The platform also provides a Student and Parent Portal, giving authorised users access to relevant academic and school information.

Available features may vary depending on the school's subscription package and enabled settings.

Developed by Paul Media Services

Paul SchoolHub is a product of Paul Media Services, a registered business providing digital, creative and technology services.

Through Paul SchoolHub, Paul Media Services aims to make practical digital solutions available to schools, helping them organise their operations and manage important information more efficiently.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify school administration through accessible and practical technology, improve the organisation of academic records, and support better coordination among authorised school users.

Our Commitment

We are committed to continually improving Paul SchoolHub by developing useful features, enhancing the user experience and providing schools with digital tools that support their day-to-day activities.

Need help? Visit the Help & User Guide for platform instructions. For school-specific questions, contact your school's administrator.
