Getting Started

A guide to accessing your Paul SchoolHub account, understanding your dashboard and getting started with the platform.

Welcome to Paul SchoolHub

Paul SchoolHub provides a central platform for managing school activities, academic records and communication. The features available to you depend on your account role, assigned permissions and your school's enabled features.

1. Logging In to Your Account

To access Paul SchoolHub, open the login address provided by your school administrator.

  1. Open your school's Paul SchoolHub login page.
  2. Enter your assigned username.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. Click the login button to access your account.
Important: School users should use the login address supplied by their school. Platform administrators use the separate platform administration login page.

2. Setting Up Your Password for the First Time

When a school administrator creates an account and provides an email address, a password setup link may be sent to that address.

  1. Open the password setup email.
  2. Follow the link provided in the email.
  3. Choose and confirm your password.
  4. Return to the school login page and sign in.

Password setup links are valid for 72 hours and can only be used once.

If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact your school administrator.

3. Resetting a Forgotten Password

If you already have an account but cannot remember your password:

  1. Open your school's login page.
  2. Click Forgot your password?
  3. Follow the instructions provided.
  4. Use the password reset link sent to your email to choose a new password.

If you no longer have access to your registered email address, contact your school administrator for assistance.

4. Understanding Your Dashboard

After logging in, you will be taken to your dashboard. It provides access to the information and tools available to your account.

The dashboard and navigation menu may differ depending on whether you are a school administrator, teacher, student or another authorised user.

  • School administrators: Access school management and administrative tools permitted by their account.
  • Teachers: Access teaching-related tools and assigned academic responsibilities.
  • Students: Access available student portal features and their own school information.
  • Platform administrators: Access platform-level administration tools.

Use the navigation menu to open the sections available to your account.

5. Registering Students

Authorised school users can register students through the student management section.

  1. Open the Students section from the navigation menu.
  2. Select the option to register a new student.
  3. Enter the student's required personal and academic information.
  4. Complete the form and submit it.

The system generates an admission number for the registered student.

Where an email address is provided, the student can receive a password setup link. The student can then use the account to access the available student portal features.

6. Registering Teachers

Authorised school management users can register teachers through the teacher management section.

  1. Open the Teachers section.
  2. Select the option to register a new teacher.
  3. Enter the teacher's personal and professional details.
  4. Provide the relevant employment and assignment information.
  5. Submit the registration form.

The system generates a staff ID for the registered teacher.

If an email address is provided, a password setup link can be sent to the teacher so they can set up their account.

7. Keeping Your Account Secure

  • Keep your username and password private.
  • Choose a strong password that others cannot easily guess.
  • Do not share your login details with other users.
  • Always log out when using a shared or public computer.
  • Contact your school administrator if you suspect someone has accessed your account without permission.
Need assistance? Contact your school's administrator for help with account access, registration or school-specific questions.
